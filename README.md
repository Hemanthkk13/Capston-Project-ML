In this histogram, I analysed the distribution of different numerical features in my Spotify dataset. The main purpose is to understand how the song features are distributed, which values occur more frequently and whether there are any unusual values or outliers."

1. Valence
Valence represents the musical positivity or happiness of a song.
The values range from 0 to 1.
From the graph, I observed that most songs have medium to high valence.
This means many songs in the dataset have relatively positive musical characteristics.
2. Year
This represents the release year of songs.
From the graph, I observed that the dataset contains songs from approximately 1920 to 2020.
Most songs are concentrated in the years after 1950.
This shows that the dataset contains songs from different generations.
3. Acousticness
Acousticness measures how likely a song is to contain acoustic instruments.
The values range from 0 to 1.
I observed that many songs have very low or very high acousticness.
This means the dataset contains both less acoustic and highly acoustic songs.
4. Danceability
Danceability measures how suitable a song is for dancing.
I observed that most songs have danceability values between 0.4 and 0.8.
This indicates that many songs in the dataset have moderate to high danceability.
5. Duration_ms
This represents the duration of a song in milliseconds.
I observed that most songs have shorter durations, while a few songs have extremely high duration values.
This creates a right-skewed distribution.
These extreme values may represent unusually long tracks or outliers.
6. Energy
Energy represents how energetic and intense a song sounds.
Its values range from 0 to 1.
From the graph, I observed that songs have different energy levels, but many songs have moderate to high energy.
This shows that the dataset contains both calm and energetic songs.
7. Explicit
This indicates whether a song contains explicit content.
0 represents non-explicit songs.
1 represents explicit songs.
I observed that most songs are non-explicit, while fewer songs are explicit.
8. Instrumentalness
Instrumentalness indicates whether a song contains vocals or is mainly instrumental.
I observed that most songs have values close to zero.
This means most songs in the dataset are not purely instrumental.
A smaller number of songs have high instrumentalness.
9. Key
Key represents the musical key of a song.
It contains values from 0 to 11.
I observed that songs are distributed across different musical keys.
This indicates that the dataset contains songs with different musical arrangements.
10. Liveness
Liveness indicates the possibility that a song was performed live.
I observed that most songs have low liveness values.
Only a smaller number of songs have higher liveness values.
This suggests that most songs in the dataset are likely studio recordings rather than live performances.
11. Loudness
Loudness represents the overall loudness of a song, measured in decibels.
I observed that most songs are concentrated between approximately -20 and 0 dB.
Very few songs have extremely low loudness values.
12. Mode
Mode represents whether a song is in a major or minor key.
1 represents major mode.
0 represents minor mode.
From the graph, I observed that major-mode songs are more common than minor-mode songs.
13. Popularity
Popularity represents the popularity score of a song, ranging from 0 to 100.
I observed that many songs have low popularity scores, while a large group has scores between approximately 20 and 60.
This shows that the dataset contains songs with different popularity levels.
14. Speechiness
Speechiness measures how much spoken-word content is present in a song.
I observed that most songs have low speechiness values.
This indicates that most songs are music-oriented rather than spoken-word recordings.
Only a few songs have high speechiness values.
15. Tempo
Tempo represents the speed of a song, measured in beats per minute (BPM).
I observed that most songs have a tempo between approximately 80 and 150 BPM.
This indicates that many songs have a moderate tempo.
16. Energy-Loudness Ratio
This is an additional feature created using energy and loudness.
I observed that most values are concentrated near zero, while some extreme negative values are present.
This indicates that the feature has an unusual distribution and may need further investigation before using it in machine learning.
